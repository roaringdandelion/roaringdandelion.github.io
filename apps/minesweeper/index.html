How to play

  1. Left-click a tile to reveal it. Your first reveal is always safe.
  2. A number tells you how many mines touch the tile, including diagonals.
  3. Right-click a hidden tile to place or remove a flag. Reveal every safe tile to win.

Power-ups

Extra Life: Survive one mine hit. Scanner: Inspect a 3 × 3 area. The World: Freeze the timer for 15 seconds once per round.
